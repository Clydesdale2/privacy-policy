Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 25, 2026 • App: Brain Spark: Match & Magic!

1. Overview

Mazzei Studios ("we", "our", or "us") develops and operates Brain Spark: Match & Magic!. We are committed to protecting user privacy, especially for families and younger players. This Privacy Policy details how data is handled when you use our mobile application.

2. What Information We Collect & Store

Brain Spark does not require you to create an account, register, or provide personal identity details (such as your real name, email, address, or phone number).

  • Local Game Progress (On-Device): Game scores, XP levels, Sparks balance, card backs, and unlocked themes are stored locally on your device using HTML5 LocalStorage and native app preferences. This data remains on your physical device and is not transmitted to our servers.
  • Network State & Internet Access: The app utilizes internet connectivity exclusively to load optional rewarded ads and fetch public font/audio assets.

3. Third-Party Services & Advertising

Brain Spark integrates Google AdMob to provide optional rewarded video ads (e.g., players choose to watch an ad to earn in-game Sparks). When requesting ads:

  • Google Play Families Policy & COPPA Compliance: Our ad requests are tagged with tagForChildDirectedTreatment: true and maxAdContentRating: 'G'. We strictly prohibit behavioral ad targeting, personalized advertising, or collecting Android Advertising IDs (AAID) from children.
  • European Economic Area (EEA) & UK Users: We utilize Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to obtain explicit user consent in accordance with GDPR and ePrivacy directives where applicable.

For more details on Google AdMob's privacy practices, review the Google Privacy & Terms.

4. Children’s Privacy (COPPA & Family Safety)

Brain Spark is designed to provide family-friendly mental entertainment suitable for players of all ages. In accordance with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play's Families Policy:

  • We do not collect personal identifiable information (PII) from children under the age of 13.
  • All advertisements displayed are strictly rated G (Family-Safe) and served through Google Play Certified Ad Networks.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

Because all progress data is stored locally on your device, you have complete control over your data. To permanently delete your game data, progress, and settings, simply clear the application's storage in your Android settings or uninstall the app.
